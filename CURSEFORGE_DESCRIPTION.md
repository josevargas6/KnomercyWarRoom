Knomercy War Room

Rated Battleground command and battlefield awareness for World of Warcraft Retail.

Command your match

The Tactical Command Board turns the current score, objectives, map state, and
friendly roster into a clear next action, the player responsible, and the
reason it matters. It supports node, hybrid, flag, orb, cart, and resource
battlegrounds.

Stay aligned

See personal defense, offense, rotation, carrying, healing, and crowd-control
assignments alongside compact team and enemy rosters. Objective tracking,
battlefield-map guidance, safe last-seen information, match history, and
After Action Review keep useful evidence close at hand.

Player-controlled, always

KWR never casts, moves, targets automatically, changes keybindings, or sends
automatic chat. Optional Quick Calls send a reviewed phrase only after your
explicit click. Facts Blizzard does not expose are shown as unknown rather than
guessed.

Get started

Open the command center with /kwr. Use /kwr verify to inspect current
battlefield evidence and addon state.

KWR Sentinel is available as a lightweight companion for teammates who want a
focused personal execution card instead of the full commander interface.
