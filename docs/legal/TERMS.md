# KWR Sentinel Terms of Service

Effective Date: 2026-07-05

KWR Sentinel is a Discord bot created for the Knomercy War Room community. It provides Discord server support, addon help, diagnostics collection, AAR collection, strategy submissions, moderation assistance, and optional AI-assisted responses.

## Acceptable Use

By using KWR Sentinel, you agree not to use the bot to:

- Harass, threaten, abuse, or target other users.
- Share illegal, harmful, hateful, or malicious content.
- Spam commands or disrupt the server.
- Upload malicious files or intentionally false reports.
- Attempt to exploit, reverse engineer, or abuse the bot.
- Request or promote cheating, botting, scripting, or automation that violates game or platform rules.

## Discord Server Use

KWR Sentinel may assist moderators by logging reports, warnings, timeouts, bug submissions, diagnostic reports, and user-submitted support requests.

Server staff may review bot submissions for moderation, support, development, and community safety.

## Addon and Game Safety

KWR Sentinel is not designed to control World of Warcraft, automate gameplay, send in-game chat, change protected keybinds, or perform protected game actions.

Any diagnostics, AARs, or battlefield logs submitted to the bot are voluntarily provided by users.

## User Submissions

Users may submit bug reports, diagnostics, AARs, strategies, screenshots, and suggestions. By submitting content, you allow the KWR team to review and use that information to improve the addon, documentation, support process, and community resources.

Do not submit private information, passwords, account tokens, API keys, or sensitive personal information.

## Availability

KWR Sentinel is provided as-is. The bot may be updated, disabled, restarted, or changed at any time.

## Enforcement

Users who abuse the bot or violate server rules may have their submissions removed, be warned, timed out, kicked, banned, or restricted by server staff.

## Contact

For questions about KWR Sentinel, contact the Knomercy War Room server owner or staff team through Discord.
