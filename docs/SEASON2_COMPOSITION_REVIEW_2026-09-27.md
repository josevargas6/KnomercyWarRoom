# Midnight Season 2 RBG composition review — 2026-09-27

This is an ordered recruiting menu for ten-player rated battlegrounds, not a
measured ten-player win-rate tier list. The queue chooses the map, so KWR puts
map-fit options first. All current-season options retain `ADVISORY_LADDER` or
`ADVISORY_PRE_LIVE` status until reviewed team-level field results exist.

## Evidence boundary

- [Blizzard's 12.1 hotfix ledger](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24296142)
  was reviewed through the September 24 PvP movement-slow changes. Tuning
  direction alone cannot establish a composition rank.
- [US RBG spec ladder](https://wowmmr.com/tier-list/rbg?region=us), snapshot
  shown September 25, and [EU RBG spec ladder](https://wowmmr.com/tier-list/rbg?region=eu),
  snapshot shown September 24, both place Discipline Priest, Balance Druid,
  Assassination Rogue, and Holy Priest among their S-band specializations.
  Arms, Devastation Evoker, Marksmanship, Unholy, Preservation, Mistweaver,
  Retribution, and Affliction have meaningful but region/hero-tree-dependent
  support. These are aggregate spec results, **not** evidence that any exact
  ten-player lineup has won more games than another.
- The previous 12.0.7 `MetaSnapshot` remains excluded from 12.1 combat-target
  influence. This review changes formation choices only; it does not turn a
  ladder ranking into individual enemy truth or tactical priority.

Every current-season shell contains ten explicit specifications and one tank,
three healers, and six damage/support slots. Automatic recruiting prefers a
current-season, map-fitting shell and uses observed roster fit before advisory
tie order. An explicit manual selection remains authoritative, including a
legacy shell. RBG spec ladders change daily; refresh this review when a later
Blizzard hotfix or sufficiently different cross-region snapshot warrants it.
