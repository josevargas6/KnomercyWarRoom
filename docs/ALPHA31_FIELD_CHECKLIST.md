# Alpha31 composition field check

Confirm `/kwr verify` reports `6.1.1-alpha.31` and candidate
`alpha31-season2-comp-review-20260927-1` before collecting evidence.
Any randomly assigned rated battleground qualifies.

1. Before queueing, open Setup Center. The unknown-map default should be
   `S2 A Balanced Control Core`; old shells should say `LEGACY`.
2. Once assigned a map, check that the first option fits that map: node split
   on node maps, carrier/return on Warsong Gulch or Twin Peaks, and objective
   fight on Eye of the Storm, Temple, Silvershard, Deephaul, or Seething Shore.
   The actual roster may make another current-season shell the automatic target.
3. Capture a screenshot of the ordered options, current roster, suggested
   recruits, and map. Note any role, specialization, or objective-job mismatch.
4. During the match capture `/kwr perf` and `/kwr verify`, and after it copy
   the AAR/export. Check that a manual LEGACY selection remains manual and is
   never called a proven current meta comp.

The spec ladder is aggregate evidence, not a ten-player result. Do not mark a
shell field-proven after a single game; keep the review diagnostic until team
outcomes and the existing performance/UI gates are reviewed.
