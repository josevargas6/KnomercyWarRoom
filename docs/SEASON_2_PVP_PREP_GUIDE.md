# Midnight Season 2 PvP Preparation

**Status:** Advisory preparation sheet. This document is not a runtime tactic,
vendor-price authority, or patch-tuning source. Recheck every `VERIFY LIVE`
item in-game before spending a non-refundable currency.

## Confirmed by Blizzard

- Patch 12.1 (Curse of Ula'tek) launched in North America on August 11, 2026.
- Midnight PvP Season 2 starts the week of August 18, 2026; Arena Training
  Grounds is available during the August 11 week.
- Earning 2,500 Conquest in Season 2 awards two Venomous Gladiator's Weapon
  Tokens.

Sources: [Season 2 schedule and rewards](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24294369/the-shadows-deepen-midnight-season-2-begins-august-18) and [12.1 launch announcement](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24294370/curse-of-ulatek-goes-live-august-11-journey-to-the-coiled-isle).

## Safe preparation

1. Cap or retain Honor only after checking the live seasonal carry-over notice.
2. On season start, accept the available weekly PvP quests before queueing.
3. Buy the two PvP trinkets first if the live set bonus is present and useful to
   the character's role.
4. Replace clearly weak slots with Honor gear, while reserving planned crafted
   non-tier slots.
5. Do not buy a Conquest weapon early solely out of habit: compare its value
   with the confirmed 2,500-Conquest weapon-token route.
6. Before crafting or converting a tier-slot item, use the live preview and
   verify the item can or cannot be converted as intended.

## Spend-order guardrails

- Pick the intended four tier slots and preferred secondary stats before buying
  Conquest pieces.
- Keep crafted items in non-tier slots unless the live conversion preview makes
  the tradeoff deliberate.
- Inspect the conversion and recrafting previews every time; never assume a
  season's behavior matches the previous season.
- Do not spend PvE upgrade currency expecting a larger PvP-scaled item level
  unless the item tooltip explicitly says it will do so.
- Reassess temporary Week 1 recommendations after the live vendors, rewards,
  and first official hotfixes are visible.
